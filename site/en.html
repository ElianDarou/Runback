Running and strength training.
Everything recorded.
Everything on your phone.

Runback is an open Android app with a Wear OS watch app. It records runs and sets, imports your old history and shows how your training develops.

Real screenshots of the app with sample data. The app itself is in German for now.

Running

Every run, complete.

Route, pace, heart rate and cadence — recorded by the phone, the watch or a chest strap. Afterwards you see every kilometre.

  • GPS route on the map, also in the background
  • Pace, heart rate and cadence over distance or time
  • Kilometre splits, pacing and consistency
  • Run type is suggested, you confirm
  • Voice and vibration cues

Strength training

Every set, traceable.

Weight and reps for each set, compared with your last sessions. So you see whether an exercise is really moving forward.

  • Tick off sets, with a rest timer
  • Each exercise compared with your last sessions
  • Estimated maximum, clearly marked as an estimate
  • Trend: rising · stable · falling · not clear yet
  • Sets per muscle group and templates

Statistics

Week by week.

Distance, time, sets and volume over four weeks, twelve weeks or everything. Personal bests lead straight to the session they come from.

Wear OS

On your wrist.

The watch app runs on its own. Leave the phone at home or take both.

  • Record runs with just the watch, with GPS and heart rate
  • Control strength training from your wrist while the phone is connected
  • Detect the end of a set and reps for selected exercises
  • If the watch switches off, what you recorded so far is kept

Your data

Stays with you.

Recording and analysis work without internet and without an account. There is no central Runback server. The only things that leave your phone are map tiles, waypoints when you plan a route, weather if you turn it on, and whatever you send to OpenRouter or your own server yourself.

LocalEverything on the device, no account.
BackupTraining data and settings as a ZIP, restorable.
ExportRuns as GPX, reports with analysis and time series.
Own serverOptional: website and API on your NAS.

Coach · optional

One suggestion, when the data supports it.

Runback suggests at most one thing per area and later checks whether it helped. If the data isn't enough, it says “not clear yet”. If you don't need it, switch it off.
